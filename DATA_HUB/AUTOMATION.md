# Automation — what runs without an LLM now

**The idea:** scripts do the repeating, mechanical work: fetching bars, resolving plans, checking
rules, testing strategies. An LLM (or you) is woken only when a pre-written trigger fires and a
real judgment call is needed. Full audits with file:line citations:
`sources/automation_audit_CALL.md` and `sources/automation_audit_REPLAY.md`.

## The CALL desk, automated (`workspace/paper/CALL/desk_check.py`)

One call runs the desk's whole checklist in the audited order, reusing the desk's own tools.
`resolve.py` stays the only thing that writes trade outcomes.

**Wakes you / an LLM (exit code 10, a GitHub issue in the workflow):**
a plan triggered, closed, expired or voided · an event on a bar under 30 min old (bars get revised)
· a reversal gets called · a counter-trend setup qualifies · data stale for >30 min or 3 failed
fetches · drawdown reaches $2,600 · the brief files change.

**Noted but quiet:** volatility stand-down switching on or off · price touching a hub level (the
hub showed levels alone don't beat random, so a touch by itself isn't worth a wake) · 15:28 ET
window close with plans still pending · hub reports over 24 h old.

**The window is computed in Eastern time inside the script** (Sun 18:00 → Fri 15:30 ET, dark 15:30–18:00),
so the old UTC-cron bug and the 2026-11-01 daylight-saving change can't break it.

**Fixed along the way:**
- The desk's data folder had been archived, so `resolve.py` silently saw no bars. Now the
  tools read `workspace/paper/CALL/live/`, a base layer of links into `data/archive` plus
  gitignored fetch deltas.
- Four runtime files the tools read were restored from the archive.
- The status card's hard-coded $240/$120 budget is now computed from current drawdown
  ($228.11/$114.05 today).
- `refresh_archive.py --fetch` now drops vendor stub bars.

**Still judgment (an LLM or you):** forming a *new* directional read and plan, and writing
findings. The audit proposes a pre-registered template for plan construction. Run it in shadow
against a placebo before it's allowed to create plans.

## The REPLAY desk, automated (`DATA_HUB/tools/walkforward.py`)

- **Parity:** fed REPLAY R1's two real orders, it reproduces the harness to the tick: +1.8949R
  and +1.8540R. That's pinned by `tests/test_walkforward_parity.py`.
- **Finding F1, the harness placebo is broken:** it reuses the real trade's absolute stop and
  target prices. When the coin picks the other side, the stop sits on the wrong side of entry,
  and the "placebo" scratches instantly at −0.0671R. So the desk's published z (+1.02) and its
  leak alarm are unreliable. `walkforward.py` uses the same *distances* from its own fill, over
  1,000 draws.
- **F2:** the "16:00 flat" exits at the close of the 16:00 bar (it's consistent everywhere, so it
  stays, labelled).
- **Search width is counted, not declared:** a rule must be registered in
  `walkforward/LEDGER.tsv` before it can run. Editing a rule changes its fingerprint and counts as
  a new trial.
- **Level placebo:** for level rules, the whole run is repeated with the levels swapped for
  random prices in the same range.

### First result: your LVN idea (OPEN_QUESTIONS #1), pre-registered and run on all 4 symbols

Rule: short the first touch from below of an LVN on the 60m-built 1-week volume profile. Stop =
target = 1 ATR from the fill. No other filters. Luck bar 1.79 (5 trials in the ledger: 4 symbols,
plus 1 for a re-registration).

| symbol | trades | win rate | payoff | avg R (after costs) | t | beats coin-flip direction | beats random levels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MES | 35 | 60% | 0.99 | **+0.18** | 1.13 | z 1.9 (p 0.03) | 10 of 10 runs |
| MGC | 18 | 61% | 1.04 | **+0.23** | 1.01 | z 1.1 | 10 of 10 |
| MNQ | 24 | 50% | 0.91 | −0.05 | −0.23 | z 0.3 | 9 of 10 |
| MCL | 29 | 52% | 0.79 | −0.09 | −0.43 | z 0.2 | 6 of 10 |
| **all 4** | **106** | **56%** | **0.91** | **+0.06** | **0.64** | p 0.035 | 35 of 40 |

**Plain English:** shorting a low-volume node on the way up beats picking a random direction, and
beats the same approach at random prices. It also made money on MES and gold. But over 106 trades
the average gain (+0.06R) is too small to tell apart from zero (t 0.64 vs a 1.79 bar). **Not an
edge yet.** It's the best-behaved level rule measured so far. Crude (MCL) is where it fails, as the
hub predicted: crude trends through levels. Full outputs: `walkforward/OQ1_lvn_fade_1w60_*`.
