# LTA Concepts: study area

Everything we learn from the **LTA Concepts 2.0** e-book (Lorenzo Corrado, "The Trading War Map")
goes in this folder. The repo is only about this book.

The book itself is not stored in the repo. The owner keeps the PDF.

## Page numbers

The owner's PDF is 304 pages, and **PDF page = the page number printed in the book**:

- p1 is the cover. p2–15 are blank. The table of contents is on p16–17.
- Chapter 1 starts on p18. The glossary is on p294–303.
- The Introduction (book pages 1–16) was removed from the owner's copy.

Pages are cited as **"book p73"**. The older `[pN]` cites in `CONCEPTS_DIGEST.md` come from the
original 314-page PDF, so subtract 10 to get the book page.
