# ANCH1 — anchored structures, backtested per symbol

Pre-registration: `HYPOTHESES.md` (committed before any arm ran). All numbers: `results/TABLES.md`
(generated by `mktables.py` from `results/*.json`, never hand-typed). Diagnostics: `diag_mgc.py`.

- Data: `data/archive/<SYM>_60m.jsonl`, 2024-10-06 → 2026-09-30. MES 11,399 / MNQ 11,400 /
  MGC 11,406 / MCL 11,041 bars after stub-bar removal. 0 duplicate and 0 backwards timestamps.
- In-sample = first 60% (split at 2025-12-12 on MGC). Out-of-sample = last 40%.
- **348 in-sample variants** searched. **24 out-of-sample confirmations**, each run once.
  Luck bar √(2·ln 24) = **2.521**.
- One exit geometry for every arm: entry next open ±1 tick, stop = level ∓ (0.5·ATR14 + 1 tick),
  target 1.5R, 12-bar time exit, flat at the session close, stop wins same-bar ties,
  one position at a time. Costs φ per round turn: MES 0.288 pts, MNQ 0.720, MGC 0.144, MCL 0.0144,
  plus one adverse tick on each side inside the fill.

---

## 1. Bottom line

**Nothing clears the bar. One result comes within 0.03 of it, on MGC, and then fails on money.**

Of 24 out-of-sample confirmations, 8 are positive and 16 negative; the best is +0.4324R and the
worst −0.2946R. The single candidate is **MGC, Fibonacci trend-failure at the prior week's golden
pocket** — t +2.705 against a bar of 2.521, which it clears, and a shift-null z of +2.49, which
it misses. §4 gives it in full, including the reason it would not have been funded.

Three things died cleanly, which is worth as much as a win:

1. **The naked-POC revisit claim is an artefact of the range, not a property of the POC.**
   Real naked POCs are revisited within 10 sessions 90.9–92.0% of the time. Random prices in the
   same session's range are revisited 89.1–89.9% of the time. The gap is **+1.5 to +2.7
   percentage points** against a pre-registered threshold of +5. The famous "~80% get revisited"
   number is true and tells you nothing: you would get it by throwing a dart at the day's range.
2. **The naked-POC magnet trade is untradeable at this account size.** It needs price at least
   1 ATR away and risks 1.5× that distance, so the $120 budget refused 207 of 207 MGC trades and
   210 of 212 MES trades. Where it did run, 195 of 215 exits were the session close — it does not
   reach the POC inside a day. Mean R was +0.0010 to +0.0066. It is a flat line, expensively.
3. **Merged-timeframe LVN confluence is too rare to measure on 60m.** Requiring an LVN on ≥2 of
   the 1-session / 1-week / 1-month / 3-month profiles within ¼ ATR yields **0.22–0.48 confluent
   levels per day** — 2 to 13 in-sample trades. Reported as unmeasurable, not as a null. The
   single-window comparison arm was measurable and lost on all four symbols.

And one negative finding is strong enough to be a rule in itself — see §5.

## 2. Per symbol — what the 60m tape says

**MES.** The flattest of the four. Every family lands between −0.11R and +0.03R out of sample.
The AVWAP wide-range-bar band fade was the best in-sample arm (+0.1149R, t 1.235) and came back
−0.0074R, t −0.073. MES F3b had a shift-null z of +2.33 but t of only +0.268 and a coin flip beat
it 10 times out of 10 — that disagreement is the shift-null's standard error shrinking on 108
trades, not an edge. Nothing here.

**MNQ.** The worst of the four for these structures. Fib continuation at the prior session's
golden pocket was the best in-sample arm in F3 (+0.1501R, t 1.862) and flipped to **−0.1984R,
t −1.969** out of sample, beaten by both placebos 10/10 — a textbook overfit sign flip. Band
continuation from the RTH open lost 0.1099R. MNQ is also where the one MGC candidate inverts:
the identical rule runs −0.2390R, t −1.932.

**MGC.** The only symbol with anything. Gold carries the single candidate (§4) and, in the
opposite direction, the project's clearest losing structure (§5). Both concern the same fact: on
MGC, trading *with* the apparent structure loses and trading against it does not.

**MCL.** Crude gave the most positive signs and the least evidence. Three of six families were
positive out of sample, but every one was small (n=20 to 44) and the halves disagree:
F2 `fade_week_first` is +0.55R then −0.30R; F3b `zigzag:golden` is +0.32R then −0.34R. That is
noise with a sign, and it matches the hub's existing finding that 44% of MCL days are trend days.

## 3. Anchoring methodologies, ranked by what they actually did

Seven anchors were tested for the AVWAP and its σ bands — the question you asked about where to
anchor. Across 4 symbols × 3 band widths × 2 warm-ups (168 in-sample arms):

| anchor | verdict |
|---|---|
| widest-range bar of the trailing 20 sessions | best in-sample on MES and MNQ; both failed out of sample |
| highest-volume bar of the trailing 20 sessions | best in-sample on MCL; failed out of sample |
| RTH session open | **unusable on 60m bars.** The session is 6–7 bars long, so a σ needs most of it. At the pre-registered 5-bar warm-up MGC and MCL produced literally zero eligible trades. A 3-bar warm-up makes it measurable and it still loses. Session-anchored VWAP needs 5m or 15m data, and this repo only holds 2 months of that |
| 18:00 Globex open | the highest-volume anchor by trade count (393–444 in-sample) and reliably negative on MGC (−0.2470R, t −5.316) |
| week open, month open | nothing, except that month-open fades lose on MGC (−0.3254R, t −4.643) |
| most recent 5-bar swing pivot | the largest sample of all (470 in-sample on MGC) and the worst: −0.2522R, **t −5.927** |

The honest summary: **no anchor beat any other anchor out of sample.** The anchor choice changed
the trade count a lot and the expectancy not at all. Where it mattered, it mattered in the
negative direction, and only on gold.

## 4. The one candidate: MGC fib trend-failure at the prior week's golden pocket

**The rule.** Take the prior calendar week's high and low on MGC. If the week closed above where
it opened, call it an up-leg and mark the retracement levels 0.618, 0.705 and 0.786 measured down
from the high (mirror for a down week). During MGC's day session (08:00–12:00 ET bars; no new
entries in the final RTH hour), if a 60m bar trades into one of those levels and **closes against
the prior week's direction**, enter at the next bar's open in that same against-the-week
direction. Stop = the level ∓ (0.5·ATR14 + 1 tick). Target 1.5R. Flat at the session close.

It is the inverse of the continuation trade everyone is taught. The continuation version of the
identical setup runs **−0.4429R, t −4.750** in sample.

| | n | avg R | t | win rate | payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| in-sample | 59 | +0.2547 | +1.976 | 47.5% | 2.07 |
| **out-of-sample** | **33** | **+0.4324** | **+2.705** | **66.7%** | **1.54** |
| both halves of OOS | — | +0.446 / +0.419 | — | — | — |
| full sample | 92 | +0.3184 | +3.170 | 54.3% | 1.86 |

**What it survives.** Direction placebo beaten 10/10. Level placebo beaten 10/10; re-run
count-matched (the fair version, because random levels get touched twice as often) it still gives
z +2.191. Positive in all three calendar years (+0.533 / +0.197 / +0.452) and in 7 of 8 quarters.
Drop the single best trade and it is +0.3054R; drop the five best and n=87 still gives +0.2507R at
t +2.469. 51 longs and 41 shorts, so it is not a disguised bet on the gold rally.

**What it fails.** Four things, and the fourth is fatal for now:

1. **The shift-null.** z +2.493 against the 2.521 bar. Against 200 circular shifts of the entry
   times with sides and geometry held fixed, this rule is at the 2.49σ point — under the bar this
   study pre-registered. It clears on t and misses on the shift-null, and where they disagree the
   pre-registration says the shift-null wins.
2. **It is not the golden pocket.** Split by ratio: 0.786 alone is n=14, +0.6541R, t +2.993;
   0.618 alone is n=16, +0.3040R, t +1.123; 0.705 alone is n=16, +0.1489R, t +0.541. The result
   rides on the deepest level, on 14 trades. "Golden pocket" is the wrong name for whatever this is.
3. **It is MGC-only.** The identical rule out of sample: MES −0.0251R (t −0.183),
   **MNQ −0.2390R (t −1.932)**, MCL +0.1592R (t +0.878). You asked for per-symbol work and this
   is per-symbol, but a structure that inverts on the nearest liquid market is not structure yet.
4. **The account could not fund it.** Median stop is **20.4 MGC points = $204 per contract**,
   max 153 points = $1,533, against a $120 risk budget. Out of sample the account took **6 of 33
   trades** and refused 27, ending at $50,392.17 with a $113.18 maximum drawdown. The $392 is real
   and it is also 6 trades. This is the same wall `RULES_AND_PITFALLS.md` already names: "MGC's
   daily-ATR stop doesn't fit at all."

**So:** the most promising thing this project has measured, and still not an edge. It needs more
span (a longer MGC 60m history), a fundable stop, and its own fresh pre-registration — not another
look at these bars.

## 5. The strongest finding is negative: do not fade gold into its own structure

Every AVWAP σ-band fade on MGC loses, from every anchor, at sizes that are not close to the line:

| anchor and band | n | avg R | t |
|---|---|---|---|
| swing pivot, ±1σ | 470 | −0.2522 | **−5.927** |
| 18:00 Globex open, ±1σ | 393 | −0.2470 | **−5.316** |
| swing pivot, the AVWAP line itself | 395 | −0.2300 | −4.861 |
| month open, ±1σ | 138 | −0.3254 | −4.643 |
| widest-range bar, the AVWAP line | 127 | −0.3368 | −4.436 |

These are in-sample numbers and they are consistent across anchors, which is what a real effect
looks like. Note what it is *not*: the continuation inverse (F1b, close through the band and go
with it) also lost — MGC `hi_vol_bar:brk2` came back −0.1664R out of sample. So the finding is not
"go the other way". It is narrower and more useful: **on MGC, a 60m bar poking a volume-weighted
band and closing back inside is not a reason to trade.** Taken together with §4, the only thing
that worked on gold needed price to close *against the prior week*, not back inside a band.

The one place the negative sign did convert is exactly §4, and that is why §4 exists.

## 6. What I would test next, in order

1. **Longer MGC 60m span.** The candidate's whole problem is n=33 out of sample. Yahoo caps 60m
   at 730 days, so this needs a different source, not a different search.
2. **A fundable stop for the MGC rule.** A resting limit at the level instead of a next-open
   market order removes the fill gap and takes the risk from ~20 points to ~0.5·ATR. Pre-register
   it as a new rule with its own ledger entry; do not re-score these bars.
3. **Session-anchored VWAP on 5m/15m**, once there is more than 2 months of it. The session open
   is the standard anchor and this study could not test it: 6 bars is not a session.
4. **The 0.786 retracement on its own, on gold, on fresh data.** n=14 at t +2.993 is either the
   real finding hiding inside §4 or the reason §4 is a fluke. One pre-registered test separates them.
