## 3. The number: gross edge required to clear zero

  A strategy on this tape at a 1.0-ATR stop must find 0.081 R of GROSS edge per trade just to pay costs

  Realistic round-turn slippage (a tick in AND a tick out) raises the bar to 0.107 R.
  Measured gross expectancy of the both-directions sample: +0.0225 R.
  Shortfall to break even at all-in cost: +0.0587 R.

  For scale: at the sample's 36.3% gross win rate on a 2R target, 0.081 R/trade is the same
  as needing 2.71 points of extra win rate at fixed payoff.
